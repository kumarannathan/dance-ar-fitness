## Overview

In this milestone, we've made significant progress in working on some of the core functionalities of the application, specifically the ability to upload, play, and share dances with realtime feedback! 

## Key Achievements

### Dance Editor

We have developed a [Dance Uploader](/dance/upload) that allows logged in users to upload a dance they have on their device and select points they want to track to make up a complete score. They can then upload the dance and scoring data to DanceAR, which will show up on their profile and allow them to share it with others!

### Dance Player

We have also developed the ability to share and play a dance uploaded by another user on your device, with realtime feedback showing how well you do on each move based on the points selected in the Dance Editor.

### Video Demonstration

We have also made a video demonstration of both of these tools being used that also includes more commentary on next steps and the future state of the product.

### Dance Battle

We have also added a Dance Battle feature that compares uploaded videos to each other and includes an AI chatbot to provide assistance.



## Next Steps

For Milestone 4, we plan on working towards the following goals:
1. Develop a more intuitive editor UI/UX
2. Add recap screens, animations, gesture detection, and out-of-frame alerts to player UI
3. Add community features to discover dances from the community
4. Update pricing pages and home pages to reflect more of the goals of the project
5. Having more content on the platform for demonstrations
